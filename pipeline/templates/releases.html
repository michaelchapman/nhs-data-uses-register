Files released over time

NHS England's Data Access Request Service (DARS) releases data to the organisations holding agreements as files. The register records each file and the month it went out. This page counts them by month.

What these charts count

  • Files released externally by DARS, by the month of release, under the agreements listed in the {{ meta.edition|edition }} edition. A file can hold a little data or a great deal, so counting files does not measure volume. Access granted in NHS England's own systems, such as its Secure Data Environment, is not included, so an agreement with no files recorded may still have had access to data.
  • They start in {{ chart_start|month }}. The register lists {{ early_files|commas }} files released from {{ stats.first_release_month|month }} to the end of {{ chart_start_year - 1 }}, against {{ first_chart_year_files|commas }} in {{ chart_start_year }} alone. Why the earlier years are sparse is not known, so they are left out of the charts rather than read as a rise in sharing. They are in the table below.
  • The last month is as complete as the others. The register reports 99% of files in the edition published the month after they were released, so {{ latest_month|month }} is not a partial month.
  • Onward sharing is not recorded. {{ stats.sublicensing|commas }} agreements permit the recipient to pass data on; nothing passed on appears here.
  • Agreements no longer in the register are not counted, though their files are on their own pages.
